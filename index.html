Background

I’m an Aerospace Engineering graduate who ended up moving into data engineering, taking the same problem-solving mindset from engineering and applying it to data. I enjoy taking messy, complicated problems and turning them into something that actually works.

Over the last few years, I’ve gone from building smaller scripts and projects to working on proper data solutions, designing ETL pipelines, working with cloud technologies, modelling data and building analytics around it. I like the parts of the job that happen behind the scenes: getting the data right, making things scalable, and figuring out how to make a system better.

I’m currently working as a Data Engineering Consultant, building experience across cloud-native data engineering and analytics. I work with technologies including Databricks, Apache Spark, Delta Lake, Python and Power BI, alongside machine learning and data modelling.
