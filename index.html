// 01_about

A bit about how I work

I'm a data engineer with a background in backend systems, which means I care about pipelines the same way I'd care about any production service: they should be observable, they should fail loudly and recover quietly, and they should never silently drop a row.

Over the last few years I've moved from writing one-off scripts to designing systems — orchestrated batch jobs, streaming ingestion, warehouse modeling, and the monitoring that tells you when any of it breaks at 3am. I like the unglamorous parts of the job: schema design, backfills, idempotency, cost.

Outside of pipelines, I write about data infrastructure and contribute small fixes to the open-source tools I rely on.
